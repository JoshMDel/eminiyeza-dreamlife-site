Local action, global goals: how the SDGs guide our work

We are not politically aligned. We are inspired by state legislation and the Sustainable Development Goals.

Insight

Eminiyeza Dreamlife is self-made and not politically aligned. What guides us is the law of the land and the Sustainable Development Goals, a shared agenda for people and planet.

Goals we can act on locally

Quality education, decent work and economic growth, sustainable cities and communities, and partnerships for the goals are all things a local organisation can move forward, one programme at a time. Our tourism, skills and community-resilience work is where those goals become concrete.

Standards we hold ourselves to

Our tone is soft but firm. We are warm with the people we serve and clear about the standards we keep: transparency, respect for community, lawful operation and service delivered with pride.

Work with us

Funders, partners and volunteers who share these standards are welcome. Send us a message and let us talk about what we can build together.

Why cultural tourism builds stronger communities

When visitors come for the heritage, nature and stories of a place, the benefit can stay with the people who live there.

Tourism

Tourism is often measured in arrivals and bed-nights. At Eminiyeza Dreamlife we prefer a different measure: what is left behind in the community once the visitors have gone home.

Heritage as a living resource

Songs, crafts, food, landmarks and the stories told by elders are not museum pieces. They are a living resource, and when they are shared with pride and respect they create work, keep traditions alive and give young people a reason to know their own history.

Hospitality is a skill that can be taught

Guiding, languages, first aid, booking and basic administration are all learnable. That is why our tourism work and our skills work are one effort, not two. A young person who can welcome a visitor in their language, keep them safe and manage a simple booking has a career, not just a job.

Tourism done well is a way of building a better tomorrow for everyone involved.

Serving with pride

Our principle is simple: anyone who is ready to serve with pride is welcome. If you would like to take part in, fund or promote cultural experiences, get in touch with us.

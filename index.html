What we do

Quality services, built around people

We offer the best quality for less: tourism-related services and practical skills that equip our youth and strengthen our communities.

Heritage & Cultural Tourism

Heritage, nature, culture, marine and astronomy tours that share local stories and create work for local people.

Youth Skills Development

From digital technology to agriculture, we equip young people with practical skills for work and enterprise.

Languages

Foreign, domestic and sign languages: French, Swahili, Dutch, German, English, Afrikaans and sign language.

Disaster Management

First aid and basic firefighting skills that make communities and workplaces safer.

Administrative & Legal Support

Administrative, legal and social interventions that help people and organisations get things done.

Local Government Mandates

Strategic services aligned to local economic development mandates and the Executive Mayor's office.

About us

Soft but firm. Independent and self-made.

Eminiyeza Dreamlife is a non-profit company focusing on tourism and cultural experiences. We are self-made and not politically aligned, inspired by state legislation and the Sustainable Development Goals.

Whether it is digital technology or agriculture, we believe each one of us can be the change we want to see in the world, regardless of our circumstances.
